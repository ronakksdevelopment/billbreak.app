About BillBreak

From 15 October 2026, UPI payments to merchants above ₹2,000 carry a 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate (MDR), deducted from what the business receives. BillBreak helps you stay comfortably compliant by splitting any total bill into the minimum number of separate UPI QR codes, each kept at or below ₹1,999.

Every QR code is a standard UPI payment link generated entirely on your device — nothing is sent to any server. BillBreak does not process payments, hold your money, or verify transactions; your customer's UPI app handles the payment exactly as it always has.

Completely free to use. No percentage charged, no subscription, no hidden fees — ever.
